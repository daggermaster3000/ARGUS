Statistics
Model

A mixed model compares WT3 with GD1 with the slide as a random effect, so the sections of one slide are not counted as independent evidence. Grouped by genotype × experiment, each experiment is fitted on its own (without the experiment term). The result is the table under the statistics; the plot can show it.

Modelled: the plotted value on the scale chosen under Statistics (“Slide tests on”). log(1 + y) and log y give a fold WT3 / GD1, the values as they are a difference.

Colours

Marker fill and edge per group (both panels), the mean ± SEM bar, the fit lines, and the text and axes. The defaults are the publication greys: WT3 dark grey, GD1 white, black edges.

Human against automated panel
Human counts by

Every human-counted section of the annotators ticked: the human count against the automated count of the positivity method chosen above, in the area the human counted (annotator 1 the whole section; annotator 2 from 50 µm before the junction line on). The same ticks also apply to the human counts everywhere else on the page.

Ratio by group: one small marker per human-counted section (automated ÷ human), one large marker per slide (the geometric mean of its sections), and the geometric mean of the slide means with its 95 % CI. The tests use slides as the unit (sections of one slide are not independent): Welch t and Mann–Whitney on the slide means of ln(ratio), and a linear mixed model of ln(ratio) on the sections with the slide as a random intercept. Each group's ratio is also tested against 1 (one-sample t on its slide means).

Methods

Methods text for the figure as it is set up now: the image analysis, the positivity method and threshold chosen, the plotted outcome (your formula, with every variable defined), the sections included and the statistics shown. It follows every change; copy it into the manuscript and edit there.

Data: the raw values behind the plot

One row per section: its design, its section variables, the raw counts of the positivity method chosen above (total, in the junction region, crossed, home, in the area the human counted) with the section's threshold, the formula's value and whether the section is in the plot. Click a column title to sort; the search keeps the rows that contain the text. The CSVs hold the numbers unrounded.

Further data. Every counted object (AF488 mask, counting method) with its position, area, distance to the junction line and side: af488_mask/<section>/objects.parquet in the run's analysis folder (mask = "kmad5"), with the thresholds in summary.json. The objects drawn in the section browser, per section: section_browser/parts/<section id>.json. The notebook's per-section outcomes: figures_official/sections_table.csv (the same as sections_table.js here). The per-method counts of this page: section_browser/data.js (written by section_browser.py).
